v3 breaks - Cloudflare Turnstile spam protection

Your current site already contains the Turnstile client and server-side verification code.

Cloudflare setup:
1. Open Cloudflare > Turnstile > your v3 breaks contact widget.
2. In Hostname Management, allow:
   - v3breaks.com
   - v3breaks.pages.dev (keep temporarily while testing)
3. The PUBLIC Site Key stays in site-config.js. It is safe to commit.
4. The SECRET Key stays in:
   Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > v3breaks > Settings > Variables and Secrets
   Variable name: TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY
   Save it as encrypted/Secret. Never commit it to GitHub.
5. After deployment, submit one real test from https://v3breaks.com and confirm it reaches your inbox.

The server checks:
- Turnstile token validity
- action = contact
- hostname matches the live request hostname
- token is valid/single-use

Keep the existing Resend settings too:
- RESEND_API_KEY = encrypted Secret
- CONTACT_TO_EMAIL = your private inbox
- CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL = your Resend sender
