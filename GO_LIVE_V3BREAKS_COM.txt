v3breaks.com GO-LIVE CHECKLIST
===============================

1) CLOUDFLARE PAGES
- Open Workers & Pages > v3breaks > Custom domains.
- Add: v3breaks.com
- Wait until Cloudflare shows the domain as active.
- Keep the existing Pages project. Do NOT create a new site.

2) TURNSTILE
- Open Cloudflare Turnstile > your contact-form widget > Settings.
- Add hostname: v3breaks.com
- Keep v3breaks.pages.dev temporarily while testing.
- Your existing TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY stays in Pages Variables and Secrets.
- Your public site key remains in site-config.js.

3) TEST
- Open https://v3breaks.com
- Test Spotify, Bandcamp, YouTube, Apple Music and SoundCloud.
- Submit the contact form once and confirm it arrives.
- Check the site on your phone.

4) RESEND
- Add v3breaks.com in Resend Domains.
- Add the DNS records Resend gives you to Cloudflare DNS.
- Wait for verification.
- Then you can change CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL in Cloudflare to:
  v3 breaks <website@v3breaks.com>
- CONTACT_TO_EMAIL can remain your private Gmail inbox.

5) CANONICAL REDIRECTS
- Once v3breaks.com works, redirect alternate hostnames to the main domain.
- Recommended public canonical URL: https://v3breaks.com/

6) PRODUCTION METADATA INCLUDED
- canonical URL = https://v3breaks.com/
- Open Graph URL uses v3breaks.com
- your chosen assets/1691.jpg share image is preserved and now uses an absolute v3breaks.com URL

- robots.txt points to sitemap.xml
